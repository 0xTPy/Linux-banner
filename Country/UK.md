Do you want to customize your Linux terminal? You are in the right place!
Here, you can display your distribution's logo or another drawing in a few commands when launching your terminal.

Steps:
  1) Open your terminal and type the following command:
     ```bash
     nano ~/.bashrc
     ```

  2) Scroll all the way down using your keyboard arrows.

  3) Once at the very bottom, skip a line and paste your ASCII art.

<p align="center">
                        =============================<br>
<b>                         /!\ WARNING /!\</b><br>
                        =============================
  </p>
On Linux, to copy and paste in a terminal, it is not CTRL + C / CTRL + V, but:
CTRL + SHIFT + C / CTRL + SHIFT + V

At the beginning of each line of your ASCII, you must type echo " ...... ".

You can look at the examples in the "ASCII-linux" folder to help you!
